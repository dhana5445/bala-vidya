THEORY · WHAT IS THIS?

A fraction shows equal parts of a whole. The top number tells how many parts we have. The bottom number tells how many equal parts make the whole.

SOLVED EXAMPLEIf a roti is cut into 6 equal pieces and you eat 2, you ate 2/6, or 1/3 of the roti.
